## 1. Executive result

The existing EvidenceLab architecture is compatible with the Host LRE route. No canonical evidence-model redesign or SQLite/Alembic migration is required for EL-LRE0.

The new operating relationship is:

```text
C2 automatic semantic collection
        +
Host LRE bounded evidence requests
        ↓
EvidenceLab verified comparative / rationale evidence
        ↓
Host versioned policy
        ↓
Host replay / evaluation
        ↓
Host production cutover
```

C2 remains active and continues to collect Trouble Brewing expert material. The new LRE lane changes **triage and handoff priority**, not the ingestion stack.

C3 remains historically valid because Q04 supplied the first accepted Drunk comparative item, but C3 is no longer the general continuation mechanism. Its reusable semantics are absorbed into the broader LRE evidence lane.

Broad Drunk acquisition remains stopped.

## 2. Host production invariant and EvidenceLab consequence

Host now distinguishes:

```text
one legal result
    -> RULE_DETERMINISTIC

multiple legal results + accepted versioned policy
    -> POLICY_READY

multiple legal results + no mature policy
    -> MANUAL_REQUIRED / POLICY_DEFERRED
```

EvidenceLab's job is therefore not to make an old heuristic better. It is to provide enough source-backed evidence for Host to define, bound and evaluate a **new versioned policy** for one decision family at a time.

EvidenceLab must never fill a policy gap by:

- inventing legal alternatives;
- treating every unchosen legal candidate as rejected;
- turning one expert choice into a global ranking;
- copying Host policy predicates into canonical evidence;
- inferring recommendation weights from outcomes;
- preserving old heuristic authority.

## 3. Canonical candidate/evidence semantics

EL-LRE0 standardizes the following vocabulary for LRE handoff.

| Relation | Owner | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `OBSERVED_CHOICE` | EvidenceLab | A source-backed historical Storyteller choice/result actually occurred. It may be observed directly or conservatively reconstructed with provenance. |
| `EXPLICIT_PREFERENCE` | EvidenceLab | The source explicitly prefers A under stated conditions. It is bounded to the stated scope and is not a global ranking. |
| `EXPLICIT_REJECTION` | EvidenceLab | The source explicitly says not to choose/use B under stated conditions. |
| `EXPLICIT_COMPARISON_LOSER` | EvidenceLab | In an explicit A-vs-B comparison, the source identifies B as the less-preferred option under the stated condition, without necessarily rejecting B globally. |
| `LEGAL_UNCHOSEN` | Host / downstream | Host proves a candidate was legal at the decision boundary but it was not historically chosen. This is **not** source-backed rejection. |
| `SYNTHETIC_NEGATIVE` | Host / ModelLab downstream | A negative candidate generated by a training/evaluation recipe. It is not historical evidence. |

Required invariant:

```text
OBSERVED_CHOICE != EXPLICIT_PREFERENCE
EXPLICIT_PREFERENCE != global ranking
LEGAL_UNCHOSEN != EXPLICIT_REJECTION
SYNTHETIC_NEGATIVE != historical evidence
```

An observed historical A may coexist with many downstream `LEGAL_UNCHOSEN` candidates. EvidenceLab does not convert those candidates into preference losers unless the source explicitly did so.

## 4. LRE policy-authorizing evidence shape

The highest-value LRE item has:

```text
fixed / bounded decision context
+ observed or explicitly preferred candidate A
+ explicit alternative B / rejected candidate / comparison loser
+ source-backed rationale
+ conditions / limitations
+ provenance / verification
```

Not every useful item must be a historical full-domain E3 case.

Two evidence shapes are valid:

### 4.1 Historical decision evidence

Use a historical `DecisionSlice` when a real decision can be reconstructed.

Preferred contents:

- pre-decision historical prefix;
- observed Storyteller choice;
- source-backed rationale;
- source-backed explicitly considered/rejected alternatives when present;
- derivation and verification;
- source/Storyteller provenance.

Host may later enrich this with the legal candidate domain. Those Host-derived legal candidates remain `LEGAL_UNCHOSEN` unless the source says more.

### 4.2 Generic expert comparative guidance

Generic guidance can authorize a **policy dimension** when it has:

- a clear decision family;
- explicit conditions;
- an explicit preference, rejection or comparison;
- rationale;
- trusted source provenance;
- primary-audio verification.

It does not need a fabricated game/reconstruction identity.

Generic guidance can justify a bounded policy predicate or evaluation feature. It cannot by itself justify a complete family-wide ranking or numeric weight.

## 6. What Host LRE supersedes

The following old execution wording is superseded:

1. **"Find an E3 PASS, then reopen C5" as the general acquisition objective.**
   E3 remains useful historical qualification language, but LRE now asks a simpler product question: what source-backed evidence is needed to define and evaluate the next bounded versioned policy?

2. **C3 as the special active downstream evidence lane.**
   Q04 remains VERIFIED historical evidence of the route's success. Future Drunk material enters LRE only when a bounded Host request exists or is collected incidentally by C2.

3. **Broad C5-specific review queues.**
   Existing C5/E3 artifacts remain historical evidence and locator material. They no longer determine global EvidenceLab priority.

4. **Requirement that policy evidence be a full historical legal-domain case.**
   Historical full-domain cases remain especially strong, but verified generic expert comparisons may establish policy dimensions when their scope is explicit.

The following are **not** superseded:

- C2 automatic collection;
- whole-game reconstruction as the primary historical collection unit;
- C2 primary-audio promotion gate;
- EL-TBGS interoperability;
- EL-ML0 boundaries;
- existing C0/C1/C3 evidence and provenance;
- existing E3/C5 audit documents as historical records.

Do not repeatedly call `prepare-next` against the exhausted fixed queue unless queue eligibility has intentionally changed.

LRE-aware triage should prioritize findings containing:

- explicit preference;
- explicit rejection;
- explicit comparison;
- named alternative;
- choice rationale;
- condition/scope limitation;
- historical observed Storyteller choice;
- evidence that a tempting alternative fails for a stated reason.

The existing `EXPLICIT_ALTERNATIVE` category remains useful. EL-LRE0 does not require a new C2 persistence schema.

## 11. Whole-game reconstruction policy under LRE

Whole-game reconstruction remains the preferred historical collection unit, but LRE does not require reconstructing an entire game before verifying useful generic expert guidance.

Use more reconstruction effort when:

- a historical decision is unusually close to policy-authorizing evidence;
- exact decision-time state materially changes the interpretation;
- Host wants replay/evaluation against the exact legal candidate domain;
- later events are needed to understand the source's rationale without hindsight leakage.

Do **not** reconstruct a whole game merely to turn generic expert guidance into a historical case.

## 13. Production-policy authorization standard

EvidenceLab never says "this policy is production-ready."

An EvidenceLab handoff may say:

```text
VERIFIED evidence supports this bounded comparison / dimension
under these source-stated conditions.
```

Host must independently decide whether that is sufficient to create a versioned policy and whether replay/evaluation proves it safe enough for production cutover.

A single verified expert comparison may justify a narrow predicate. It does not justify:

- a full family ranking;
- numeric weights;
- a player-count rule not present in the source;
- extrapolation to unmentioned candidates;
- extrapolation across scripts;
- fallback to retired heuristics.
